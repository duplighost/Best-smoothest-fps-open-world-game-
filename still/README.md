# STILL

A house, and what is under it. Five floors down.

**Headphones. Darkness. Alone.** The sound is the game — its breathing is
information, your own footsteps are a confession, and the silence means it
is listening.

## Play

Any static server (`node ../tests/serve.mjs` from the repo, then
`/still/`), or drag this folder onto Netlify. No build, no assets —
everything is synthesized when you arrive.

**WASD** walk · **mouse** look · **E** touch things · **F** your light

There is no run button. There is no health bar. Your heartbeat is the
health bar.

Every floor tells you its one rule, in writing on the wall. The rules are
all easy. Following them is not. Progress is saved at each floor; being
taken only sends you back to the stairs.

The last thing the game asks of you is one key press. Take your time.
